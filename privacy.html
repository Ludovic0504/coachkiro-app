Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Who we are

Coach Kiro Publisher ("the App") is a personal publishing tool operated by an individual creator based in France. Contact: jean.limonta06@gmail.com

2. Data we access

When you sign in with TikTok, and with your authorization, the App accesses:

  • Your basic TikTok profile (username and avatar), through the user.info.basic scope, to show which account is connected.
  • The ability to publish videos to your account, through the video.publish scope, only when you confirm a post.

3. Data we store

The App stores only the access token needed to keep your session connected. Videos and captions are sent to TikTok at the moment you publish and are not kept by the App afterwards. We do not collect data about other TikTok users.

4. How we use data

Data is used solely to let you publish your own videos to your own TikTok account. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Retention and deletion

The access token is deleted when you disconnect your account or when it expires. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security and permissions > Manage app permissions). To request deletion of any data, write to the contact address above.

6. Security

Access tokens are kept private and are never published or shared. We take reasonable measures to protect them from unauthorized access.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to object to its processing. Contact us at the address above to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available on this page.
